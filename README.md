### 🔬 Core Research Interests
- **Autonomous Systems & Aerospace Software**: Fault Detection, Isolation, and Recovery (FDIR), closed-loop onboard replanning, and CubeSat/LEO constellation resilience.
- **Uncertainty Quantification (UQ)**: Evidential Deep Learning (EDL), epistemic vs. aleatoric separation, and out-of-distribution (OOD) anomaly arbitration.
- **Formal Methods & Safe RL**: Control Barrier Functions (CBF), hard runtime physical state invariant verification, and fail-closed safety governors.
- **Physics-Informed Modeling**: Causal inference, state-space thermal-electrical orbital simulation, and counterfactual trajectory optimization.
