## 🚀 Featured Research Publication

### 🛰️ **[AstraHeal: Uncertainty-Aware Counterfactual Planning for Autonomous Spacecraft Fault Recovery](https://github.com/madankalyan2211/AstraHeal)**
> **Published with CERN / Zenodo (DOI: [`10.5281/zenodo.22233081`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22233081))**  
> *Targeted for IEEE Aerospace / AIAA Intelligent Systems*

- **The Problem:** Spacecraft in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) and deep-space regimes face catastrophic subsystem anomalies during prolonged communication blackouts. Conventional FDIR systems revert to blind Safe Mode, prematurely ending science operations.
- **The Innovation:** AstraHeal unites:
  1. **Dirichlet Evidential Bayesian Inference** to isolate epistemic from aleatoric uncertainty.
  2. **Zero-Mutation Digital Twin Counterfactual Lookahead** simulating parallel recovery trajectories across 3,000s horizons.
  3. **Deterministic Physical Safety Governor** enforcing strict hardware invariants ($T_{\text{batt}} \le 46^\circ\text{C}$, $V_{\text{bus}} \ge 22\text{V}$, $|I_{\text{batt}}| \le 40\text{A}$, $\text{SoC} \ge 15\%$).
  4. **Communication-Aware Autonomy Arbitration** governing onboard intervention during occultation.
- **Empirical Validation:** **0 executed unsafe actions** and 0 governor bypasses across 609 candidate evaluations; 100% preservation of science observation capability.
- **Artifacts & Code:** [Open Source Repository on GitHub](https://github.com/madankalyan2211/AstraHeal)
